What AI actually is. Without the hype or the panic.

LLM, pretraining

What AI actually is: AI is trained on the entire public web.

Example: FineWeb open dataset contains ~12 trillion words across many disciplines (chemistry, psychology, sociology, history, and more).

AI processing time: Months.
Human reading time: ~91,000 years (nonstop, no sleep, no breaks).

✅ Use AI for this. You can't do it yourself.

⚠️ AI only responds with what's already been written, said, taught, and repeated.

LLM, post-training

What AI actually is: AI is software. The human-like tone in its responses came from post-training with human feedback.

Example: When AI responds “my honest recommendation”…

Under the hood: AI learned this phrasing during training, when humans rated confident-sounding responses higher.

⚠️ Tone ≠ sincerity

⚠️ Response ≠ accuracy.

✅ Read AI's response as a substantive opinion, sometimes counter-argument to what you proposed, and see what it has to offer.

LLM “Hallucination”

What AI actually is: AI predicts based on mathematical calculations. It has no consciousness.

Example: When AI responds with incorrect answers to your prompts.

Under the hood: It predicts the statistically most plausible word, which isn't always the true one. Or it links to citations that don't actually exist.

⚠️ AI's claims & citations ≠ correctness

✅ Use AI to collect data fast. Use that time you saved to read and work out your brain.

LLM, sampling

What AI actually is: AI's answers vary. Same prompt, different answers every time.

Example: Ask AI the same brainstorming question twice in the same AI tool with the same model. You'll get different answers.

Under the hood: AI doesn't pick the “best” word, it rolls weighted dice from the most likely options.

⚠️ The first answer is just one sample. Not “the” answer.

✅ Use AI to see different angles on the same question. The variability shows you the range of classic approaches before you build your own.

LLM, statelessness

What AI actually is: AI chats don't share memory by default.

Example: You've been working on your essay in one chat for days. You can no longer tell if your structure makes sense. Open a fresh chat. Paste it in. Ask AI to analyze it.

Under the hood: The base AI model is “stateless”, it has no memory between chats. Without that memory, AI reads everything you give it as if for the first time.

⚠️ New chat ≠ continued conversation

✅ Use AI to break the curse of knowledge. It sees what you can't unsee.

LLM, knowledge boundary

What AI actually is: AI's knowledge ends somewhere. With or without web search, it works from what already exists.

Under the hood: AI lives in the past. Either its training cutoff, or whatever's already been published online. It can't access what hasn't been written yet.

⚠️ Web search = gives AI access to the latest information and what humans have already written.

⚠️ It cannot create in the true sense of creativity

How You Approach Tasks and Life

Perceiving (P): keep options open, figure it out as you go.
Thrives without structure. Tends to have trouble organizing ideas and narrowing down options.

Judging (J): plan first, then build.
Thrives with structure. Tends to stick with one plan and miss possibilities.

How AI Can Help

Perceiving (P): Use AI to help you organize and see structure, then commit.

Judging (J): Use AI to surface what you didn't consider and broaden your perspective, then choose.

How You Take In Information

Intuition (N): Sees patterns, reads between the lines.
Thrives in ambiguity and big-picture thinking. Tends to overlook concrete details and what's right in front of you.

Sensing (S): Focuses and dives into details, facts.
Thrives with specifics and real evidence. Tends to miss patterns and underestimate “what could be”.

How AI Can Help

Intuition (N): Use AI to ground you in real structure and guide you to read details in depth.

Sensing (S): Use AI to surface what your details add up to and find the patterns you're missing.
